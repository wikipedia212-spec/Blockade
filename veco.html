How to play

Goal

→

Fill big squares with the same color to clear them and score points. Drag pieces from the Next area or Storage onto the board — drop anywhere inside a square and it auto-places into the first free field.

Storage

→

You have 6 storage slots in the left panel. Drag any piece into an empty slot to save it for later — useful when the current piece doesn't fit anywhere yet. You can also drag a stored piece back onto the board.

White joker 👑

×4 → +100

White pieces (with an upside-down crown) are jokers — they match ANY color. Fill a whole square with 4 jokers for a massive +100 bonus and a full-screen flash!

Powers

+ – ★

Occasionally special tiles appear next to normal pieces:
💧 turn a field into a joker
+ add the missing color into an empty field
– remove a color from a field
★ fill the whole square with its color

The droplet also works on pieces in Storage — turns them into jokers.

Combos, levels & modes

Combo x3 +8

Clearing squares back-to-back builds a combo bonus that doubles: +4, +8, +16, +32... Each level unlocks a new color, and the background changes.

vs

Try HEX (hexagons with 6 fields) or Time Race (blitz mode with countdown timer, +5s per cleared square) from the main menu.
